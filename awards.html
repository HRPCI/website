Honorary Recognition

Awards & Recognitions

National-level honorary recognitions for outstanding social contribution, integrity, and leadership.

The Human Rights Protection Council, India (HRPCI) confers national-level awards and honorary recognitions to acknowledge individuals and institutions who have demonstrated outstanding commitment to social service, education, human rights, public welfare, and nation-building. These awards are conferred to honor integrity, dedication, ethical leadership, and long-term contribution to society. HRPCI awards are purely honorary and social recognitions.

Purpose of HRPCI Awards

  • To recognize exceptional service and social contribution
  • To encourage ethical leadership and responsible citizenship
  • To highlight positive role models in society
  • To inspire others toward community service and nation-building
Major Awards

Recognitions Conferred by HRPCI

🏅

Bharat Gaurav Samman

One of the most prestigious recognitions conferred by HRPCI, honoring individuals who have made significant contributions toward national development, social harmony, education, or public welfare.

🕊️

Human Rights Peace Award

Recognizes individuals or organizations working actively for human rights awareness, peace-building, social justice, and protection of human dignity.

🌟

Lifetime Achievement Award

Conferred upon individuals who have devoted a substantial part of their life to social service, education, public welfare, or community development with lasting impact.

🎓

Honorary Doctorate Award

A symbolic and honorary recognition for outstanding social contribution. It is non-academic and not equivalent to any university degree. Learn more →

🇮🇳

Pride of India Award

Honors individuals who have brought positive recognition through excellence, innovation, and dedication in their respective fields of service.

Eligibility for Awards

  • Minimum 5–10 years of notable contribution in the relevant field
  • Demonstrated positive impact on society or community
  • Strong ethical background and public credibility
  • No proven criminal or unethical record

Nomination & Selection Process

  1. Nomination — By individuals, institutions, or HRPCI members. Self-nomination may be permitted; submission does not guarantee selection.
  2. Screening — Preliminary screening of nominations by the HRPCI Secretariat.
  3. Evaluation — Assessment by an internal selection or advisory committee based on merit, contribution, impact, and ethics.
  4. Final Approval — Approval by the authorized governing body of HRPCI.

Recognition & Felicitation

  • Formal felicitation during seminar, conference, or award ceremony
  • Presentation of award, citation, and certificate
  • Optional media coverage and organizational acknowledgment

Fees & Transparency Policy

Any fees, if applicable, are strictly towards:

  • Administrative processing and documentation
  • Event organization and venue arrangements
  • Ceremonial materials, certificates, trophies, and logistics
Important: HRPCI does not sell awards or degrees. Payment of fees does not guarantee selection. All recognitions are conferred based on merit and committee approval.

Ethical Standards & Withdrawal Policy

HRPCI reserves the right to withdraw any award or recognition if:

  • False or misleading information is discovered
  • The award is misused for illegal or unethical purposes
  • The recipient brings disrepute to the organization or society

Final Disclaimer

All awards and recognitions conferred by HRPCI are honorary in nature. They do not imply governmental, statutory, or academic authority and should be represented truthfully and ethically.
